02A VISION WORTH BUILDING

For ministry leaders,

Technology should never become the focus.
People should.

Propel is not simply a proposal for a website, app or database. It is a ministry architecture built around one idea: every digital touchpoint should make it easier to know people, care for people and help people take a meaningful next step.

When the public website, mobile experience, giving, groups, Growth Track, serving, kids, events, prayer, communication, partner access and leadership tools share one connected vision, technology begins to disappear into the background.

“The strongest ministry technology is the technology people no longer have to think about.”

The mission stays visible. The systems simply help carry it.

THE MINISTRY TECHNOLOGY PROBLEM

Your people experience one church.
Your technology should too.

The challenge is rarely a lack of software. It is the friction created when every important ministry moment lives somewhere different.

Every additional disconnected tool creates another place for a person—or a follow-up—to fall through the cracks.
01Separate public website + forms
02Giving disconnected from people + care
03Guest follow-up living in spreadsheets + texts
04Groups, volunteers + events in different systems
05Leaders without one clear view of ministry health
