BUILT FOR PARTNER-SUPPORTED MINISTRY

More than donor records.
A complete partnership experience.

Designed for missionaries, evangelists, traveling ministers and organizations where partnership is central to the mission. Partner Platform is a premium add-on and can be configured around the needs of your ministry.

Partner Management

Profiles, households, contact details, relationship history, notes, tags, segmentation, giving history, partner status and personalized follow-up.

Giving & Financial Visibility

Recurring partnerships, one-time gifts, designated support, projects, monthly totals, trends, statements and partner giving history.

Messaging & Donor Notifications

Targeted email, text and in-platform messaging, partner updates, giving notifications, reminders and segmented communication.

Partner Portal

A secure partner-facing experience for giving information, ministry updates, prayer, courses, projects, resources, downloads and impact.

Engagement Tools

Help partners respond, pray, watch updates, engage with campaigns, submit requests, celebrate wins and stay connected to the mission.

Courses & Training

Offer partner-only courses, video training, lessons, ministry resources and structured learning experiences with progress visibility.

PDFs, Books & Downloads

Give partners access to downloadable books, PDFs, devotionals, reports, workbooks, ministry resources and any other digital item you choose to provide.

Prayer & Ministry Connection

Partner prayer requests, ministry prayer needs, testimonies, praise reports and meaningful ways to stay connected to what God is doing.

Campaigns & Projects

Special initiatives, missions projects, outreach, fundraising goals, progress updates, designated support and visible impact.

Analytics & Reporting

Active partners, partnership totals, new partners, retention, recurring giving, engagement trends, campaign performance and leadership reporting.

Receipts & Statements

Give partners organized visibility into giving history, downloadable records and year-to-date information where supported.

Automation & Workflows

Welcome sequences, follow-up workflows, partner milestones, recurring-gift events, staff notifications and expanding intelligent automation.
